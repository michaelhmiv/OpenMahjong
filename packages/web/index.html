Open Mahjong

Free forever. Open source mahjong.

Open Mahjong is a web app for playing Riichi, Hong Kong, MCR, and Taiwanese mahjong against the CPU, each variant with its own real scoring. No account is needed to play.

The app includes 52 interactive tutorial lessons played on the real table — 11 for riichi, 10 for each other variant, and an 11-lesson American training track — and a guided mode that offers hints with explanations during CPU games. It also includes score calculators and a scorekeeper for in-person games in all four variants.

Signing in with Google is optional. Signed-in players can save their game history and settings to their account.

Open Mahjong is free and open source under the AGPLv3 license. The source code is on GitHub.
