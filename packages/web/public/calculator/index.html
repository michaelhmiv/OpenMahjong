Mahjong score calculators

Score tools for mahjong played in person. A scorekeeper keeps a session's scores, hand ledger, and settlement; the hand calculator works out a single hand. No account is needed to use them.

Scorekeepers

Riichi scorekeeperSeats and scores for a full session, with riichi deposits, the repeat counter, every hand in a ledger, corrections, and the final settlement. Hand values are entered by han and fu, or scored from the tiles.
Hong Kong Old Style scorekeeperSessions scored in fan, with your table's minimum fan and payment split recorded with the session.
MCR scorekeeperSessions scored in fan, against the eight-fan minimum.
Taiwanese scorekeeperSessions scored in tai, with the table's base and per-tai amounts recorded with each hand.

Single-hand tools

Riichi hand calculatorOne hand, no session: choose the winner and how they won, enter han and fu, and see who pays what. Nothing is recorded.
American helperEnter the hand value printed on your own card, and the app applies the standard payment rules and keeps the session.
