Privacy Policy

Open Mahjong — last updated: November 16, 2026.

Playing as a guest

Games you play as a guest, your settings, and your game history are stored only in your browser on your device. They are not sent to our servers.

Accounts

If you sign in with Google, we store your Google account identifier, the display name and profile photo from your Google profile, the date you created your account, records of your finished games (date, game length, placement, score, the game's move record, and game statistics), and your settings, so they follow you between devices. We do not receive or store your email address or your Google password.

Cookies

We use one cookie to keep you signed in. Advertising cookies are described in the Advertising section below.

Advertising

This site uses Google AdSense to display ads. Google and its partners use cookies to serve and measure ads. The ads shown on this site are non-personalized: they are based on the content of the page and general location, not on your visits to other sites. Google describes how it uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can review and control ad settings at adssettings.google.com.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Your information is processed by Google (sign-in and advertising) and Railway (hosting) only to operate the service.

Deletion

Settings, then Delete account, permanently deletes your account and everything stored with it. Guest data can be removed by clearing your browser's site data.

Children

Open Mahjong is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
