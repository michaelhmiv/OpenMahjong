Hong Kong Old Style rules

Hong Kong Old Style is scored in fan. A hand needs at least three fan to win, and its fan total converts into the amounts the other players pay.

The tiles

  • The set has 144 tiles: four copies of each of the 34 ordinary kinds — 27 numbered kinds (1 to 9 in characters, circles, and bamboo) and seven honors (the four winds and three dragons) — plus eight bonus tiles.
  • The eight bonus tiles are the four plants (plum, orchid, chrysanthemum, bamboo plant) and the four seasons (spring, summer, autumn, winter), numbered 1 to 4.
  • The 1 and the 9 of a numbered suit are its terminals. An honor has no number and can only match copies of itself.

The winning shape

  • A complete hand is four sets and a pair. A set is a sequence of three numbers in one suit, a triplet of one kind, or a quad.
  • A complete shape is not enough on its own: the hand must also reach the table's fan minimum before it may win.

Fan and the minimum

  • The score of a winning hand is counted in fan. Each pattern a hand contains, and each circumstance of how it was won, earns a stated number of fan, and the fan are added together.
  • This table requires at least three fan to win. A complete two-fan hand must keep playing.
  • Winning on a tile you drew yourself earns one extra fan. Holding no bonus tiles at all also earns one fan.

Flowers and seasons

  • A bonus tile is never kept as part of your hand. When one appears, it is placed face-up beside your hand and you take another tile instead.
  • Bonus tiles you hold are counted in the hand's score when you win.

Payments

  • Fan convert to base points: three fan is 8 base points, rising through 16, 24, 32, 48, 64 and 96 to 128 at ten fan. Fan are capped at ten.
  • On a discard win, the table's payment split decides the discarder's share: the discarder pays two shares and each other opponent one, or the discarder pays all three shares.

The match

  • A full match runs four rounds in order: East, South, West, and North. The dealer holds the East seat for the hand.
  • When the dealer wins, the same dealer deals again and the hand is a repeat. After any other result, including a drawn hand, the deal passes to the next player.
  • Every player starts a match with 1,000 points. Payments only move points between the four players, so the scores always add up to 4,000. A shorter match — only the East round, or East and South — uses exactly the same hand rules.
